Kord — hear one instrument at a time
====================================

WHAT IT DOES

  Search for a song. Kord finds it, works out the chords and the key, and
  splits it into separate parts — guitar, bass, drums, vocals — so you can
  listen to just the one you're learning.

  No account needed. Nothing to sign up for.


TO INSTALL

  1. Unzip this download (double-click it) if it isn't already.
  2. Double-click  Install.command
  3. Follow the two lines it prints.

  That's it. Kord lives in your Applications folder and opens in its own
  window like any other app. The first launch takes a few seconds while it
  starts up.


IF MACOS SAYS IT CAN'T BE OPENED

  Right-click (or Control-click) Install.command, choose Open, then click
  Open in the box that appears. You only have to do this once.

  This happens because the app isn't signed with a paid Apple developer
  account. It's not a sign anything is wrong.


USING IT

  Type a song name in the box at the top and pick it from the list. The
  first time you open a song it takes a couple of minutes — it's listening
  to the whole track to work out the chords.

  Once it's ready, scroll down and press "Separate the parts". That takes
  another couple of minutes. Then you can switch each instrument on and off,
  or press "only" next to guitar to hear it on its own.

  Everything plays together, so switching the bass on halfway through drops
  it in exactly where the song already is.

  If a song isn't findable, "Open a file" lets you use an audio file you
  already have.


WHERE YOUR STUFF IS KEPT

  ~/Library/Application Support/Kord

  Songs you've opened, the analysis, and the separated parts. Deleting the
  app doesn't delete these.


IT NEEDS THE INTERNET

  To look a song up and fetch its audio. Once a song has been analysed and
  separated, playing it back works offline.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

  There's a log at:
  ~/Library/Application Support/Kord/kord.log
